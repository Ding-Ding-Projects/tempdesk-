# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, critique work without personal attacks and protect the privacy of other participants. Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults and disclosure of private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer with only the information necessary to investigate. A maintainer involved in a concern should refer it to another maintainer. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or take other proportionate action. They should limit disclosure of the report to those handling it.
