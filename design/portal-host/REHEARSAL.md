# Design rehearsal

Two local prototype frames were rendered in headless Microsoft Edge using separate temporary profiles and synthetic publication and network fixtures. No service was contacted. The desktop review frame is 1120 × 900 and shows native-ownership verification as an example state. The narrow bilingual frame is 360 × 1500 to include the below-fold recovery controls and the verified guest URL. Their scene addresses are `Desktop.dc.html?scene=review&lang=en` and `Narrow.dc.html?scene=partial&lang=bi`. The physical desktop and user browser profile were untouched.

The first narrow frame cut off the right side because a minimum browser window centered the fixed-width reference. The revised board anchors its own 360 px container, aligns labels and controls in a bounded grid, and wraps the bilingual recovery action. The failed management row now explicitly reports the failed check, while the guest link remains available. Both reference frames were rendered again after shortening the preview fixture labels.

Stageshow's craft, framing and review references informed the framing and inspection. The product action and its outcome take priority over decorative filler. These are local design frames, not screenshots of the packaged wizard. Hosted canvas synchronization, a complete stage-by-stage film and production acceptance remain pending.
